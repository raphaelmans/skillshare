# Use an optional committed Paper Manifest

`paper-openspec init` explicitly creates or refreshes one committed `PAPER.md` at the repository root that owns `openspec/`; schema v1 contains exactly one Paper Target, and initialization performs structural inventory rather than semantic parity or historical reconstruction. The manifest inventories current-state pages and canonical surfaces, excludes Exploration and Archive pages from normal coverage, and may cache Paper page or node IDs only as non-authoritative hints paired with human names. Other slices remain usable when it is absent, must consume it when valid, and verify only entries with concrete Staleness Evidence; an existing Paper-mutation or code-mutation approval also covers mechanical maintenance of affected entries, while read-only slices never write the manifest and elapsed time alone never triggers re-audit. A mutating slice stops before its Paper mutation when overlapping manifest edits cannot be preserved, and it reports partial completion rather than success when required post-mutation manifest maintenance fails.
