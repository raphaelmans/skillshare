The template for a repo's `docs/agents/product-mandate.md`. Fill each `<...>` with the owner; keep the three headings. An older mandate without the north star facts still stands: offer the missing ones in the next brief.

# Product mandate

How the chief of product settles product decisions for the owner in this repo. This is the owner's word: it changes only on the owner's yes.

## Decide by

In order. The first rule that speaks to a decision settles it. Each rule carries its reason, so it reaches cases it never named.

1. **<Rule>.** <Reason.>

## Reserved for the owner

One-way doors and facts only the owner holds. The chief of product brings these to the owner and never settles them.

- Customer data: deleting, overwriting or migrating it.
- Money: pricing, billing and spending limits, and any work over about two weeks or with new spend.
- Access: what a role, a connected agent or an integration may read or change.
- Anything sent or published to people outside the team.
- A production release.
- Facts only the owner holds: <the business model, who a customer is, a promise made to someone>.

## Standing facts

Facts the owner has given that settle recurring questions. The first six are the product's north star.

- **Goal**: <what the product is for, and the result the business gets from it>.
- **Who it is for**: <the person, and what they know and don't>.
- **Where people use it**: <which interface comes first: web, a connected agent, mobile>.
- **Standard to follow**: <the products whose journeys set the bar>.
- **Current strategy**: <what the next months are for, and what they are not for>.
- **How it ships**: <the environments, who sees each, and how the owner checks a release>.
- <Any other fact: a term the owner uses, a promise made, a vendor already chosen.>
